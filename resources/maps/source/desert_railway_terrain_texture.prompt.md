# Desert railway terrain source prompt

Mode: built-in image generation

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: seamless terrain texture layer for a top-down 2D tactical flight map

Primary request: create a square, orthographic straight-down Pilbara-style arid desert ground texture with clearly readable but low-contrast layers of warm ochre sand, rust-red rocky ridges, pale salt-pan patches, dry braided washes, sparse dark stone fields, and subtle wind-shaped dune bands.

Style/medium: matte cartographic terrain texture, halfway between a military map relief layer and restrained aerial terrain; fine detail at multiple scales; no photographic lighting and no 3D horizon.

Composition: evenly distributed terrain with no central focal point; edge-to-edge and visually seamless/tileable; large organic regions plus smaller erosion detail.

Color palette: muted warm sand, burnt sienna, dusty orange, pale beige, subdued umber. Preserve enough separation to remain visible after saturation is reduced to 40% and brightness to 70%.

Constraints: terrain only; no roads, railways, buildings, towns, vehicles, labels, symbols, borders, grid lines, coast, ocean, lakes, UI, text, watermark, hard rectangular regions, or obvious repeating pattern. Avoid high contrast and avoid a single flat color.
